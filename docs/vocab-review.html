Four things I could not settle

  1. red_wine is two products. It appears inside both Pastes (a 10 g red-wine paste sachet) and Vinegars & cooking wines (red wine vinegar). Needs two keys; I don't know which recipes mean which.
  2. Generic versus specific. Recipes say oil, milk, yoghurt, cheese. The house holds sunflower and olive, skimmed and semi and oat, Greek 0% and Greek style. A decrement can't be right until each resolves to one.
  3. Whether the five food bundles get split. Everything tagged in a bundle depends on it. Until they're split those ingredients can't be decremented after a cook or asked about before one.
  4. Chicken by cut. Three pack keys exist and recipes say all of “chicken”, “chicken breast”, “bone-in skin-on chicken thighs”. Some of that is ambiguous in the source text.
